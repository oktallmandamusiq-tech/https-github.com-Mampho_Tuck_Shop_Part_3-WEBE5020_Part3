About Mampho Tuck Shop

Mampho Tuck Shop is a fictional small retail business created for the WEDE5020 website project. The shop is imagined as a convenient stop for residents, learners, parents and nearby workers in Mamelodi East.

The product range focuses on items people often need during the day: a snack between classes, a cold drink, bread for home or a basic cleaning product.

Our mission

To provide affordable everyday essentials with friendly, reliable service in a clean and welcoming environment.

Our vision

Who we serve

Local residents, learners, parents and workers who want quick access to everyday products.
